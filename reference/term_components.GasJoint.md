# How a Joint Score-Driven Filter's Parameters Divide

Returns the entries of
[`term_components()`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/term_components.md)
of every term of the filter, named and indexed in
[`term_params()`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/term_params.md)
of the joint filter, followed by one entry per cross loading of the
term.

## Arguments

- term:

  A built
  [`GasJoint()`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/GasJoint.md).

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of entries, as
[`term_components()`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/term_components.md)
documents.
