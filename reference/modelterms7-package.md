# modelterms7: Model Terms as Objects for Formula-Based Modeling

Model terms as 'S7' objects: the mapping from a data frame to a
design-matrix block, the penalty attached to the block's coefficients,
and the metadata that a fit reads, with a formula interpreter that
recognizes term constructors by what they return rather than by name, so
that user-defined terms work in formulas without registration. The terms
include parametric and penalized terms (ridge, lasso, elastic net, SCAD
and MCP), random effects, smooths and tensor products, nonlinear terms,
break-points, score-driven filters and regime switching, and a
constructor for a censored response. Part of the 'statmodels7' toolkit.
