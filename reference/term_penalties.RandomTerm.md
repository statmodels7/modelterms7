# The Penalties of a Random-Effect Term

One entry over the whole block where the effects of a group are read
together, and one per within-group column where they are independent.

## Arguments

- term:

  A built
  [`RandomTerm`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/RandomTerm.md).

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A list of entries, as
[`term_penalties`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/term_penalties.md)
documents.
