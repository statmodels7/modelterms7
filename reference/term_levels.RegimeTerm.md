# Levels of a Regime Term

The regime means, ordered by construction: the first level and the
cumulative sums of the positive gaps.

## Arguments

- term:

  A
  [`RegimeTerm`](https://statmodels7.github.io/modelterms7/reference/RegimeTerm.md).

- psi:

  The term's parameters.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A numeric vector of length `k`.
